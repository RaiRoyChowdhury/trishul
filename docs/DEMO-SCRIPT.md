# Judge demo script (about 5 minutes)

Before you start: two phones or a phone + laptop, both signed in (two different accounts), one trusted contact = your own Telegram, Wi-Fi on, app language Bengali. Have the film ready as a backup.

1. **Problem (20 s):** "Women and teens in Kolkata face unsafe moments before, during and after journeys. TRISHUL is one ecosystem, not six apps."
2. **Home (20 s):** show the dashboard in Bengali, switch to English and Hindi. Point out the "prototype, not a guarantee" notices.
3. **AI Fake Call (60 s):** open AI Call, pick the "বাবা" caller, scenario auto-rickshaw, Start. Accept; speak in Bengali; the AI answers aloud. Say: "Clearly labelled AI simulation; voices only with the owner's consent."
4. **Safety Map (60 s):** show reports and the legend (hollow = unverified). Tap a spot, submit a report. Compare routes and read the explanation. Say: "We never call a street safe; fewer reports is not safety."
5. **Secret-Code SOS (60 s):** show your own phrase, Test mode, then say it live: countdown, cancel once; trigger once for real and show the Telegram message and the honest status line.
6. **Walk Together (45 s):** phone A creates a journey, phone B requests, A accepts. Show that a teen account cannot see adult journeys.
7. **No-Touch (30 s):** show the capability panel and shake test. State the limit: foreground only; native Android app is the next step.
8. **Coming Next (20 s):** Shakti Network, Evidence Vault, StalkSense… clearly labelled Planned.
9. **Close (15 s):** "Honest about limits, privacy by design, Bengali-first."
